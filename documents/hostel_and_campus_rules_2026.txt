================================================================================
CAMPUS RESIDENCE, HOSTEL & STUDENT CONDUCT RULES (2026-2027)
PROCTORIAL BOARD & CHIEF WARDEN OFFICE
================================================================================

1. HOSTEL CURFEW & MOVEMENT TIMINGS
1.1 Daily Gate Closure:
    - Main campus gate and all residential hostel entry points close strictly at 9:00 PM.
    - Entry after 9:00 PM requires biometric log, security desk sign-in, and notification sent to the Chief Warden.
1.2 Night-Out & Leave Passes:
    - Night leave passes must be submitted on the Student ERP portal at least 6 hours in advance (before 5:00 PM).
    - Parental SMS / call verification is mandatory before night pass clearance.

2. HOSTEL MESS & DINING HOURS
2.1 Breakfast: 7:30 AM to 9:15 AM
2.2 Lunch: 12:15 PM to 2:15 PM
2.3 Evening High Tea & Snacks: 5:00 PM to 6:15 PM
2.4 Dinner: 8:00 PM to 9:45 PM
Note: Meals are not served in hostel rooms except for sick students with medical permission slips.

3. CAMPUS FACILITIES & TIMINGS
3.1 Central Library:
    - Circulation Desk: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM (Monday to Saturday).
    - Reading Rooms: Open till 10:00 PM daily (Open 24x7 during Mid-Term and End-Term exams).
3.2 Campus Health Center:
    - 24x7 emergency doctor and nursing staff available in Residential Block A ground floor.
    - Campus Ambulance Hotline: 0120-2845-999.
3.3 IT & Wi-Fi Services:
    - High-speed campus Wi-Fi available across academic blocks and hostels.
    - Portal credentials and password resets: IT Desk, Computer Center Ground Floor.

4. STRICT ZERO-TOLERANCE ANTI-RAGGING DIRECTIVE
4.1 Any form of ragging, physical harassment, verbal abuse, or teasing is a non-bailable criminal offence.
4.2 Immediate consequence: Immediate expulsion from the hostel and institute, followed by lodging of a Police FIR.
4.3 24x7 Proctorial Anti-Ragging Squad Hotline: 1800-180-5522 / proctor@campusai.edu
