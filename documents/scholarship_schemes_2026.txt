1. UP POST-MATRIC STATE SCHOLARSHIP (FEE REIMBURSEMENT)
1.1 Category Eligibility:
    - SC/ST Students: Family annual income from all sources must not exceed INR 2,50,000.
    - General / OBC / Minority / EWS: Family annual income must not exceed INR 2,00,000.
1.2 Benefit: Up to 100% reimbursement of tuition fees directly credited to the student's Aadhaar-linked bank account.
1.3 Mandatory Requirements:
    - 75% minimum biometric attendance recorded in institute logs.
    - Income Certificate issued by Tehsildar (valid for current fiscal year).
    - Caste and Domicile Certificate of Uttar Pradesh.
1.4 Hard Copy Submission Deadline: 30th November 2026 at Scholarship Window 4.

2. AICTE PRAGATI SCHOLARSHIP FOR GIRL STUDENTS
2.1 Eligibility:
    - Female students admitted to 1st year B.Tech or 2nd year through Lateral Entry.
    - Family income must be less than INR 8,00,000 per annum.
    - Maximum two girl children per family.
2.2 Scholarship Amount: INR 50,000 per annum for stationery, books, laptop, and academic equipment.
2.3 Portal: National Scholarship Portal (NSP) - Last Date: 15th December 2026.

3. CAMPUS MERIT-CUM-MEANS (MCM) SCHOLARSHIP
3.1 Institute-funded tuition assistance for high academic achievers:
    - Branch Rank 1 to 3 with Semester CGPA >= 9.20: 50% tuition waiver for the following semester.
    - Branch Rank 4 to 10 with Semester CGPA >= 8.75: 25% tuition waiver for the following semester.
3.2 Conditions: No disciplinary remarks, ragging complaints, or attendance condonations.
