================================================================================
CAMPUS TRAINING & PLACEMENT POLICY (ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027)
TRAINING & PLACEMENT CELL (T&P)
================================================================================

1. PLACEMENT ELIGIBILITY TIERS
1.1 Tier 1 (Dream Companies / CTC > 10 LPA):
    - Minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 7.50 with no history of active backlogs.
    - Mandatory 80%+ attendance in Pre-Placement Training (PPT) sessions.
1.2 Tier 2 (Core Engineering / CTC 6 to 10 LPA):
    - Minimum CGPA of 6.50 and no more than 1 dead backlog cleared previously.
1.3 Tier 3 (Mass Recruiters / IT Services / CTC 3.5 to 5.5 LPA):
    - Minimum CGPA of 6.00 or 60% in 10th, 12th, and B.Tech.

2. ONE STUDENT, ONE JOB OFFER POLICY
2.1 Once a student receives an offer letter from any visiting recruiter, their registration is frozen for all companies in the same or lower tier.
2.2 Exception (Super Dream Upgradation):
    - A student placed in Tier 2 or Tier 3 is eligible to appear for Super Dream companies offering CTC >= INR 14.0 LPA (minimum 100% CTC increment).

3. SEMESTER INTERNSHIP GUIDELINES (8TH SEMESTER)
3.1 Students securing 6-month industry internships must obtain a formal No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the T&P Cell and Head of Department.
3.2 Fortnightly attendance and work log must be attested by the company industrial mentor.

4. UPCOMING RECRUITMENT DRIVES (FALL 2026)
- TCS Digital / Ninja Drive: 1st December 2026 (Eligibility: CGPA >= 7.0)
- Cognizant GenC Next: 10th December 2026 (Eligibility: CGPA >= 6.5)
- Infosys Specialist Programmer: 18th December 2026 (Eligibility: CGPA >= 7.5)
