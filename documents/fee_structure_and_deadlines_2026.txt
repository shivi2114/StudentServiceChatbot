================================================================================
CAMPUS FEE POLICY, PAYMENT PROCEDURES & DEADLINES (ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027)
FINANCE & ACCOUNTS DEPARTMENT
================================================================================

1. ACADEMIC TUITION FEES (B.TECH PROGRAMS)
1.1 Semester Tuition Fee: INR 65,000 per semester (INR 1,30,000 per academic year).
1.2 Examination Fee: INR 4,500 per semester (included in ERP semester invoice).
1.3 Student Activity & Lab Development Fee: INR 3,500 per annum (payable in Odd Semester).

2. HOSTEL & MESS FEE SCHEDULE
2.1 Double Occupancy (Air-Cooled): INR 42,000 per semester.
2.2 Triple Occupancy (Air-Cooled): INR 34,000 per semester.
2.3 Mess Security Deposit (One-Time, Refundable): INR 5,000.
2.4 Mess Advance Food Charges: INR 18,000 per semester (4 meals per day including evening tea).

3. PAYMENT METHODS & CHANNELS
3.1 Online Payment (Preferred):
    - All fees must be deposited through the official Student ERP Fee Portal using Net Banking, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), or Credit/Debit Card.
    - Zero transaction surcharge is levied for UPI and RuPay debit card payments.
3.2 Demand Draft (Offline Exception):
    - In exceptional circumstances, a Demand Draft (DD) in favor of "CampusAI Institute of Technology" payable at city branch may be submitted at Accounts Counter 2.
    - Cash transactions are strictly prohibited at all fee counters.

4. DEADLINES & PENALTY POLICIES
4.1 Even Semester 2026 Fee Deadline: 15th December 2026.
4.2 Grace Period with Late Fee:
    - 16th December 2026 to 31st December 2026: Late fine of INR 100 per day.
4.3 Portal Deactivation & Roll Number Hold:
    - After 31st December 2026, unpaid accounts will experience temporary ERP portal access suspension, and examination admit cards will not be issued.

5. FEE REFUND POLICY
5.1 Withdrawal requested 15 days or more prior to the officially notified start of classes: 100% tuition refund minus a processing fee of INR 1,000.
5.2 Withdrawal within 15 days of class commencement: 50% tuition refund.
5.3 Withdrawal after 30 days of class commencement: No fee refund permissible.
5.4 Caution money deposits are 100% refundable upon submission of clearance certificate (No Dues).

6. ACCOUNTS OFFICE CONTACT
- Location: Main Administrative Block, Windows 1 to 4.
- Counter Hours: Monday to Friday: 9:30 AM to 3:30 PM.
- Email: accounts.finance@campusai.edu
