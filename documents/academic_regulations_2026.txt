CAMPUS ACADEMIC REGULATIONS & EXAMINATION ORDINANCE (ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027)
OFFICE OF THE DEAN ACADEMICS & EXAMINATION CONTROLLER
================================================================================

1. MANDATORY ATTENDANCE REGULATIONS (THE 75% RULE)
1.1 Every registered student must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each registered course (lectures, tutorials, and practical laboratory sessions).
1.2 Condonation of Attendance (Medical / Official Ground):
    - A student having attendance between 60.0% and 74.9% may apply for attendance condonation on verified medical grounds or official college representation in sports/hackathons.
    - Medical certificates from a registered medical practitioner must be submitted to the Class Mentor and Dean of Student Welfare within 7 working days of returning to campus.
    - Maximum condonation permissible is 15%. Under no circumstances can a student with less than 60.0% aggregate attendance be condoned.
1.3 Debarment (Year Back / Detained):
    - Students with attendance below 75% (or below 60% after valid medical condonation) are strictly debarred from taking End-Semester Examinations in that subject.
    - Debarred students must re-register for the course during the summer semester or subsequent academic year.

2. EXAMINATION & EVALUATION SCHEME
2.1 Total marks per theory course: 100 Marks.
    - Continuous Internal Assessment (CIE): 30 Marks.
      * Best of two Mid-Term Exams: 20 Marks.
      * Quizzes / Assignments / Case Studies: 5 Marks.
      * Regularity & Classroom Attendance: 5 Marks.
    - End-Semester University Examination (ESE): 70 Marks.
2.2 Passing Criteria:
    - A minimum of 35% marks in End-Semester Examination (25 out of 70).
    - A minimum of 40% aggregate marks in CIE + ESE combined.

3. BACK-PAPERS & REVALUATION
3.1 Back-Paper Examination:
    - Students who fail to obtain the passing grade may register for Back-Paper examinations through the Student ERP portal.
    - Back-paper registration fee: INR 1,500 per subject.
    - Eligible students can carry up to 4 active back-papers into the next academic year. Failing in more than 4 core subjects leads to academic detention (Year Back).
3.2 Result Revaluation:
    - Students unsatisfied with their semester grade may apply for Answer Script Re-checking / Revaluation within 15 days of online result declaration.
    - Revaluation fee: INR 1,000 per subject (refundable if grade increases by more than 15%).
